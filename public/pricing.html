What does the estimate leave out?+

Domain registration, hosting, paid software, subscriptions and any applicable VAT. Multilingual sites, e-commerce, booking systems, complex integrations and migrations need a separate review and quote. No ongoing support subscription is added automatically.

Can I keep it really basic?+

Yes. The basic option is a complete small website. A clear written brief, final supplied content and consolidated feedback keep the scope contained. Ownership, responsive layout and a written handover are always included.

What counts as a revision?+

One consolidated set of feedback on the agreed work. New features, extra pages or a different brief change the scope and are quoted before work continues. A meeting is time to discuss decisions; a revision round is time to implement them.

Does exploring include custom design?+

The exploration option adds discussion time and one revision round. Select “A visual direction of your own” separately if you want bespoke design production. You can also choose custom design with a clear written brief and no meetings.

What happens after I choose?+

We confirm your content, technical needs, timing and scope, then agree a written quote before work starts. The calculator is a guide to that conversation. It does not reserve time, take payment or create a contract.
