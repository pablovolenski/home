03 / A few things I believe Independent, on purpose.

The web
can still
be ours.

I.

Ownership should be ordinary.
Your domain, your content, your accounts. Access isn’t a favour, and leaving shouldn’t mean starting again.

II.

Tools should come with explanations.
What they cost. Where the data goes. What happens if you change your mind. Trade-offs belong in the conversation.

III.

Automation needs a reason.
No AI added by default. If it serves a clear purpose, we discuss its limits, data use and human oversight first.

IV.

Small is a way of working.
You speak with the person doing the work. Decisions stay close to the people who have to live with them.

Not a promise of perfect independence.
A practice of making more deliberate choices. *
